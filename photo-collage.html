How to order a photo collage

Upload the selected photographs through our online order form and mention the approximate dimensions or orientation you prefer. We will review the request and confirm the available collage format, final dimensions, price and courier charge before payment. Sizes, layout options and prices are not assumed until confirmed.

Choose and prepare your photographs

Use clear original files wherever possible. Choose images with the people and important details visible, and avoid crops that cut off faces or key parts of the picture. If you have a specific layout in mind, describe it when submitting the order.

Pricing and delivery: Collage dimensions, layout options, print finish and pricing are confirmed individually before payment. Courier availability and charges depend on the destination PIN code.
