Refund & Replacement Policy

Photo prints and collages are personalized products made using the photographs and specifications submitted by the customer.

Damaged, defective or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective or materially different from the confirmed specifications, contact us promptly with your order reference and clear photographs showing the issue. We will review the case and communicate an appropriate replacement or other resolution.

Incorrect files or customer-provided details

Please review the selected photos, crop, orientation, size and delivery address before confirming your order. Issues caused by an incorrect file or detail supplied by the customer may not qualify for a free replacement.

Order cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel. Once personalized printing or dispatch has started, cancellation may not be possible. Any refund decision will be communicated after reviewing the order status and applicable law.

Privacy Policy

PhotoPrint Services collects the information you submit through the order form, which may include your name, contact details, delivery address, order selections and uploaded photographs.

How information is used

  • To receive, prepare and deliver your photo-printing order.
  • To contact you about order details, payment confirmation or delivery.
  • To resolve customer service issues and maintain necessary order records.

Photo handling

Uploaded photographs are used for fulfilling the requested order and are not intended for public display, portfolio use or marketing without your permission. Access should be limited to people who need it to process the order. Please do not upload highly sensitive images or information that is not required for your order.

Storage and sharing

Order information and files may be processed using the online form and its associated service providers. Information may be shared with a courier or service provider only as needed to complete the order. Records may be retained for operational, legal or dispute-resolution needs.

Your choices

For questions about your submitted information or a request concerning your uploaded photos, contact us through the order page and include your order reference. We will review the request subject to applicable record-keeping requirements.
