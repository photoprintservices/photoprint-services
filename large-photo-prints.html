Large prints for portraits and landscapes

A larger photograph can make a favourite memory more visible on a wall, desk or in a keepsake display. The best format depends on the original image, its resolution, the intended viewing distance and the dimensions requested.

How to order a large photo print

Upload the original high-resolution photograph through our online order form and mention the approximate dimensions you need. We will review the file and confirm available sizes, print finish, image suitability, final price, packaging and delivery charge before payment. Do not assume a particular size is available until it has been confirmed.

Image quality matters for larger prints

Use the original full-resolution file whenever possible. Avoid screenshots and messaging-app compressed copies. A file that looks clear on a phone may not retain the same detail when enlarged, so we may need to review image suitability before confirming the order.

Pricing and delivery: Available dimensions, finish, packaging and pricing are confirmed individually before payment. Courier availability and charges depend on the destination PIN code.

Frequently asked questions

Which large print sizes are available?

Available dimensions depend on the requested format and are confirmed individually before payment.

Can I use a photo received on WhatsApp?

An original high-resolution file is preferred. Messaging-app copies may be compressed and may not be suitable for enlargement.

Can I order a large portrait or landscape print?

You can enquire about either. Share the original image and requested approximate dimensions so suitability can be reviewed.

When do I pay?

Wait for confirmation of the available dimensions, finish, image suitability, final amount and delivery charge.
